## Proposed navigation and missing capabilities

Dashboard, startup management and registration queue, project management and project review, execution stages and satisfactory-performance review, funding/payment monitoring, stopped projects, social-impact evaluation, reports, decision history, account settings. Group or consolidate overlapping pages in final owner review. New management and stopped-project workspaces require design and contracts; do not assume existing implementations or approve the proposed navigation merely because screenshots were reviewed.

Stoppage: Aineh alone decides stoppage when execution cannot follow approved plan; report and notify actual contributors, record their authenticated instructions. Exact approved choices for attributable unspent share: refund, contributor-selected replacement, explicit delegation of replacement choice to Aineh. No automatic settlement, deemed consent, unlocked organization, plan amendment, or invented balance formula. Organization executes authorized real payment/refund/transfer; Moon monitors. Aineh does not hold project funds, directly pay startups, or issue official tax certificates.

## Amended execution requirements (DEC-0005)
Full verified funding of the approved total project target is mandatory before Aineh explicitly authorizes execution and determines the official start date. No lower start threshold. Startup designs the operational and financial plan (stages, schedule, outputs/KPIs, budgets, payment amounts/timing, project-specific advance if needed); Aineh reviews and approves it. Complete fulfillment of stage obligations is required for satisfactory-performance approval; no partial approval or progress-proportional stage settlement. Actual organization payment and reconciliation remain distinct; approved advances are separate and their release/offset details remain open. No amendment during execution.

Add an explicit start authorization queue for fully funded projects, separate from plan approval/publication/payment.
