## Proposed navigation

Dashboard; projects; create project; execution stages; reports/evidence; funding and receipts; payments to Moon; performance/impact; startup profile; support; settings. Amended DEC-0007 requires exactly one active user per startup; no user/access management menu or subordinate accounts. Preserve startup acceptance authority of Aineh; single-user replacement process must be specified and revoke old access without deleting history. No assumption of legal representative equals account holder without verified evidence.

## Lifecycle and outstanding contracts

Startup submits project and proposes operational plan, stages, schedule, outputs/KPIs, stage budgets, payment amounts/timing and optional advance. Aineh reviews, assigns one registered organization, approves/publicizes project. Full verified funding and Aineh's explicit start authorization and official start date are required before execution; organization pays startup directly under approved stage/advance conditions. Startup sends report and evidence, Aineh approves satisfactory performance only upon complete fulfillment of stage obligations, organization records actual payment, Moon monitors. Approved execution plan cannot change during execution. If impossible, Aineh decides stoppage and participant instructions govern attributable unspent funds. No lower start threshold, partial stage acceptance, progress-proportional settlement, fee, payout cap, advance recovery, automatic approval, automatic payment, fabricated KPI or financial value. Define exact transitions, consent, report corrections, and financial control contracts before coding.

## Amended execution requirements (DEC-0005)
Full verified funding of the approved total project target is mandatory before Aineh explicitly authorizes execution and determines the official start date. No lower start threshold. Startup designs the operational and financial plan (stages, schedule, outputs/KPIs, budgets, payment amounts/timing, project-specific advance if needed); Aineh reviews and approves it. Complete fulfillment of stage obligations is required for satisfactory-performance approval; no partial approval or progress-proportional stage settlement. Actual organization payment and reconciliation remain distinct; approved advances are separate and their release/offset details remain open. No amendment during execution.

## Complete project intake and stage reporting amendment (DEC-0004/0005)
Startup designs the complete operational and financial plan and coordinates it with Aineh outside Moon, before registering a complete project. No in-system negotiation/co-design or accepted incomplete project. Submit a report and evidence for every approved stage. Aineh approves the report, triggering automatic publication of its authorized representation; Aineh confirms final operational completion, independent of organization settlement.
