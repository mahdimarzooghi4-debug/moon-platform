## Authority matrix
- Moon admin: define registered organizations; approve and activate companies; monitor all project activity and record/reconcile information within the approved remit; record three revenue sources and actual fund activity. No project or performance approval, official certificate issuance or custody/payout of project funds.
- Aineh: sole creative house; startup acceptance, project/plan review and approval, select exactly one registered organization, approve project publication, authorize start after full verified funding and determine official start date, assess complete satisfactory performance, approve stage reports for automatic publication, approve final operational completion, sole stoppage decision when approved plan cannot be executed. No bank settlement or certificate issuance.
- Startup: propose project, stage schedule, outputs/KPIs, budgets, payment amounts/timing and optional project-specific advance; execute approved plan; report actual outcomes and evidence. No self-approval or amendment during execution.
- Company: ordinary registration/approval/activation, project participation, own verified payments/receipts, permitted reports and official tax certificates, contributor instructions if stopped. Fund follows same ordinary company flow if participating; no privileged path.
- Organization: receive funds directly into official account; direct stage payments to startup following applicable approved conditions; record actual financial evidence; legally authorized issuer officially issues applicable certificates. No extra project acceptance or veto.
- Individual contributor: mobile-only real SMS OTP, no account/panel, participation and authenticated stoppage instructions. Public site freeze remains.

## Shared information and financial integrity
Use authoritative versioned backend facts; independent approval, execution, report publication, payment, reconciliation, certificate and closure states. A UI route, localStorage record, callback alone or sample metric is not proof. Do not collapse full funding target, stage budget, actual receipts or payouts. Preserve immutable ledger and audit, deduplicated events, external evidence, correction lineage, and historical access/financial records. Separate project funds held by organization, Moon operating revenues, fund capital exclusively supplied by Moon, and fund income attributable to Moon. No invented percentages, automatic allocations, withholding, investment execution, or fund privileged payments.

Proposed cross-panel projections (subject to exact field-level visibility): registration decisions to applicant and authorized reviewers/admin; published projects to eligible users; verified receipts and performance decisions to appropriately scoped participants, startup, organization, Aineh and Moon; full report submissions only to authorized reviewers/submitter, approved/public representations separately; official certificates only to company, authorized issuing organization and appropriately scoped Moon oversight; stopped project report public without private identity, instructions scoped to actual contributor and authorized handling staff. Do not copy sensitive evidence or full beneficiary records across all panels. Unknown values must remain unknown.

Amended DEC-0007: multi-user delegation only Moon/Aineh/organization; exactly one active panel user per company/startup, no subordinate workspace. ## Business decision queue (no defaults)
1. Project plan and execution: complete intake validation/correction handling, date representation and remaining start prerequisites, response to delay and funding shortfall, report correction and stage activation.
2. Financial release: advance level and release prerequisites, recovery/offset; stage payment amount/cap (partial acceptance prohibited), actual payout authorization, evidence, reconciliation.
3. Stoppage: attributable unspent-share calculation, inter-organization transfer and recipient eligibility, nonresponse, authenticated instruction lifecycle, tax consequences.
4. Closeout: final report and satisfactory-performance evidence, financial settlement, residual funds, permitted reopening if any. Aineh final operational approval is already approved.
5. Identity and organizations: official account verification, single-user replacement, principal delegation, sensitive permission catalog, issuer authorization.
6. Revenue/fund and reporting: actual contracts, recognition and reconciliation, publication/visibility, aggregation and evidence, legal tax eligibility and official issuance mechanism.

## Owner-approved complete-project intake, stage publication and operational closeout (2026-11-28)

Before project registration in Moon, the startup designs the complete operational and financial plan and coordinates/reviews it with Aineh outside the system. Only complete projects are registered: stage definitions, deliverables/KPIs, schedule, budgets, payment amounts/timing and any project-specific advance terms must be established in that plan. Moon is not a negotiation, co-design or incomplete-proposal development workspace. Retain formal Aineh approval/publication of the submitted complete version and all previously approved funding/start controls. A technical form draft is not an accepted registered project; its exact handling is not a new business approval. No in-execution amendment. Financial/advance terms are project-specific; no universal offset, recovery, percentage or payment formula is approved. Actual payment and reconciliation remain independent.

Reports must be published for each stage defined at project registration, tied to the approved stage/version. Startup submits actual stage performance and evidence; Aineh reviews and approves the report and complete satisfactory performance under existing rules. Approval of the stage report authorizes automatic publication of the approved publishable representation, without another Moon or organization approval. Record report version, approving actor/time and actual publication outcome; never expose private beneficiary identifiers or protected evidence. Report publication and actual payment are distinct facts; do not imply publication proves settlement or automatically pays.

After actual publication of each stage report, send SMS only to actual financial contributors of that same project (companies and individuals), derived from authoritative confirmed participation records, not all platform users or contributors of unrelated projects. SMS contains project name, stage title and report link; no fabricated delivery. Deduplication per contributor/report is proposed; exact recipient identity, contact resolution, delivery/idempotency and correction policy remain Technical. Existing stoppage notification remains project-scoped under DEC-0005. Final-report SMS is a proposal, not independently approved.

Aineh confirms final operational completion after all approved stages are complete. Operational completion and financial settlement by the organization remain independent; Moon records/monitors both. No automatic settlement or inferred financial closure from operational approval. Final report/financial closeout exact evidence, residual balances, exceptions and reopening rules remain to be specified. No public-site or runtime changes authorized.
