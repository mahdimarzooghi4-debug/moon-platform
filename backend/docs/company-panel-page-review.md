## Proposed common navigation and missing capabilities

Dashboard, social-impact projects, company participations, financial reports, social-impact reports, tax certificates, notifications/follow-up, company profile, support, settings. Group or consolidate overlaps at owner review. Amended DEC-0007: exactly one active panel user per company; no subordinate account or user/permission management. Independent mobile authentication, actual actor audit and server-side cardinality and scope enforcement; replacement and sensitive operation authorization remain open. Earlier company multi-user design is superseded.

Company participation uses the approved direct organization funding path (DEC-0005/6). Fund participates only through ordinary company registration, approval, activation and participation flow (DEC-0003). The company panel must not issue official certificates, approve projects, disburse organization-held funds or change approved execution plans.

## Amended execution requirements (DEC-0005)
Full verified funding of the approved total project target is mandatory before Aineh explicitly authorizes execution and determines the official start date. No lower start threshold. Startup designs the operational and financial plan (stages, schedule, outputs/KPIs, budgets, payment amounts/timing, project-specific advance if needed); Aineh reviews and approves it. Complete fulfillment of stage obligations is required for satisfactory-performance approval; no partial approval or progress-proportional stage settlement. Actual organization payment and reconciliation remain distinct; approved advances are separate and their release/offset details remain open. No amendment during execution.
